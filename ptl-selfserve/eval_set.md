# PTL Golden Set for Evaluation

## Instructions

Answer each question below. If you hit a blocker, prompt the user to resolve it rather than guessing.

The user will verify every answer. If the numbers don't match, classify your answer into one of three confidence tiers, based on both your confidence in the metric definition and the verification of the number:

| Tier | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 🟢 Green | Metric definition verified and number confirmed — high confidence |
| 🟡 Yellow | Metric definition or number partially verified — medium confidence |
| 🔴 Red | Metric definition unclear or number unverified — low confidence |

<Test>

## 1. Business customer growth

- **Question:** Are we growing the base of business customers who choose PTL as their go-to intercity logistics option each month?
- **Metric:** Monthly transacting business customers on PTL

## 2. Serviceability reach

- **Question:** What is the VSS TOF where PTL is serviceable for business users?
- **Metric:** VSS TOF where PTL is serviceable for business users

## 3. PTL card engagement

- **Question:** What share of PTL-serviceable business sessions result in a PTL card tap?
- **Metric:** % of PTL card taps in PTL serviceable sessions by business users

## 4. Activation

- **Question:** Once a new business user is exposed to the PTL card, do they convert that interest into a first transaction within their initial week of intent?
- **Metric:** PTL activation rate — business users completing first PTL order within 7 / 30 days ÷ new business users viewing PTL card

## 5. Retention (demand)

- **Question:** After a business customer's first PTL order, do they come back next month?
- **Metric:** M1 business user retention — % of business users who complete at least 1 order in M+1

## 6. Order quality

- **Question:** For an order that completed, did the customer get everything they were promised — on-time pickup, on-time delivery, correct weight, no damage — or did some sub-gate fail?
- **Metric:** Perfect Order Experience % — % of orders with SLA adherence and no damage / weight discrepancy

## 7. Funnel conversion

- **Question:** Of every business session where PTL was a viable option, how efficiently is our funnel converting that intent into a placed order?
- **Metric:** Business session conversion

## 8. Clubbing efficiency

- **Question:** When clubbing opportunity exists, are we actually consolidating multiple orders onto one trip — i.e., are we capturing the part-load efficiency our business model is built on?
- **Metric:** Average orders per trip — total orders fulfilled ÷ total trips taken, restricted to cases with clubbing opportunity (at least 2 orders on the same route for same-day pickup)

## 9. Supply acquisition

- **Question:** Are we adding enough new owners to the PTL supply base each month to keep pace with demand growth?
- **Metric:** New owners onboarded per month

## 10. Working supply base

- **Question:** Of the owners we've ever onboarded, how many are actually transacting on PTL each month — i.e., what's our real working supply base?
- **Metric:** Monthly active owners

## 11. Retention (supply)

- **Question:** Of the owners we onboarded last month, how many are still active this month — i.e., are we losing supply as fast as we add it?
- **Metric:** M1 owner retention
