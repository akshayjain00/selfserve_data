The real finding in this whole section: NPS clearly declines and Detractors % clearly rises with BHK size, across nearly every city — bigger, more complex moves are harder to execute well. The aggregate "Overall NPS" metric hides this entirely. Read the 4+BHK column with real caution — Ahmedabad shows NPS of -100.00 there, which is a small-sample artifact (very few rated orders, one or two detractors swings the whole cell), not a real signal. This is exactly the thin-cell problem the reference wireframe's own-baseline banding was designed to solve — worth building that properly before this section is read as anything more than directional.
